# Project REIGNITE: A Revival Strategy for Ola Electric

**From India's boldest EV bet to its most trusted mobility brand.**

## Overview

This repository contains The Big Brand Theory. It is a comprehensive business, marketing and operational turnaround strategy for Ola Electric. Despite early dominance and a massive 1M unit manufacturing capacity, Ola currently faces critical challenges. These include severe service deserts, software glitches and a widening gap between marketing hype and customer reality.

This strategic deck outlines a rigorous roadmap to shift Ola's positioning from a tech hype machine to a reliability engine. It specifically targets the rapidly growing 50,000 Cr INR Indian EV market.

## The Core Challenge

Ola's primary issue was never ambition. It was execution without accountability. Rapid sales growth outpaced infrastructure and led to deep customer dissatisfaction. First-time EV buyers experienced brake lag, software glitches and distant service centers. This systematically destroyed trust before word-of-mouth could turn positive.

## Strategic Pillars

Our proposed strategy is rooted in four foundational pillars requiring operational excellence and technological integration:

* **Service First:** Activating 1,500 service hubs within 18 months and introducing a guaranteed 4-hour SLA to permanently resolve the service desert.
* **Radical Transparency:** Launching a Public Quality Dashboard to openly track live defect and resolution rates. This will earn back the trust of gig workers and first-time buyers.
* **Bharat Expansion:** Deepening market penetration with localized UI in 12 regional languages, Kirana-network charging infrastructure and WhatsApp-first booking.
* **Ecosystem Monetization:** Shifting the business model to view hardware as an entry point. We introduce OLA CARE subscriptions and Battery-as-a-Service for delivery fleets to generate high-margin recurring revenue.

## The WAPAS AAO Campaign

To communicate this operational shift, the strategy proposes a bold and brutally honest marketing turnaround:

* **The Confession:** A CEO-signed open letter in print acknowledging past execution failures and outlining concrete fixes.
* **Real Riders Docuseries:** Unscripted raw footage of real customers replacing polished high-budget ads to build authentic credibility.
* **Service Guarantee:** A 30-day money-back pledge to force competitors into a defensive position and signal absolute confidence in the new operational standards.
